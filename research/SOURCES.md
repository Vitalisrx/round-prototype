## Research method and exclusions
Public search returned some irrelevant results. Those were discarded rather than counted as research. Direct official/expert/paper source pages were used for the observations above. No claims of 10,000 researched studies, measured conversion uplift, neurotransmitter optimization, market-size certainty, or guaranteed virality are made.

The prototype's 50 activities, 60 formats, timing model, 18-reward catalog and pilot thresholds are authored product choices. The 10,000-row founder matrix is generated from those choices and explicit context variations. It is not 10,000 independent studies or opinions from real founders.
